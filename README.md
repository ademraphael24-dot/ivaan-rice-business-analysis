# ivaan-rice-business-analysis
Business operations Analysis and Performance dashboard for IVAAN RICE LTD


IVAAN RICE LTD. – BUSINESS OPERATIONS ANALYSIS

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing the business operations of IVAAN RICE LTD. through data analysis and interactive data visualisation.

The objective is to understand business performance, monitor financial indicators, identify operational patterns, and provide insights that can support informed business decisions.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Evaluate overall business performance.
- Analyse income, expenses, and profitability.
- Monitor monthly financial trends.
- Identify potential areas for operational improvement.
- Present business insights through data visualisation.

🛠️ Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Data Visualisation
- Data Analysis
- Power BI

📊 Key Performance Indicators

- Total Income
- Total Expenses
- Total Profit
- Monthly Income Performance
- Monthly Profit and Expenses Trends

🔍 Analytical Focus

The analysis examines the relationship between business income, expenses, and profitability while exploring monthly performance patterns.

💡 Business Value

The dashboard provides management with a visual overview of business performance and supports the identification of areas requiring further investigation and improvement.
