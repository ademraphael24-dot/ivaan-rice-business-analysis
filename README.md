# ivaan-rice-business-analysis
Business operations Analysis and Performance dashboard for IVAAN RICE LTD


IVAAN RICE LTD. – BUSINESS OPERATIONS ANALYSIS

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing the business operations of IVAAN RICE LTD. through data analysis and interactive data visualisation.

The objective is to understand business performance, monitor financial indicators, identify operational patterns, and provide insights that can support informed business decisions.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Evaluate overall business performance.
- Analyse income, expenses, and profitability.
- Monitor monthly financial trends.
- Identify potential areas for operational improvement.
- Present business insights through data visualisation.

🛠️ Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Data Visualisation
- Data Analysis
- Power BI

📊 Key Performance Indicators

- Total Income
- Total Expenses
- Total Profit
- Monthly Income Performance
- Monthly Profit and Expenses Trends

🔍 Analytical Focus

The analysis examines the relationship between business income, expenses, and profitability while exploring monthly performance patterns.

💡 Business Value

📈 Key Business Insights

This analysis explores the financial performance of IVAAN RICE LTD., focusing on income, expenses, and profitability.

The dashboard provides insights into:
- Monthly income performance.
- Monthly profit and expense movements.
- Overall financial performance.
- Changes in business profitability.

 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the observed performance indicators, management can:

- Monitor monthly expenses to maintain healthy profit margins.
- Investigate months with unusually low income.
- Identify factors contributing to changes in profitability.
- Use monthly performance trends to support operational planning.

📌 Conclusion

This project demonstrates how data analytics and visualisation can help businesses monitor performance, identify areas requiring attention, and support informed decision-making.
